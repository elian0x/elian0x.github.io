Hi, I'm Elian.

This website is a collection of technical writeups based on personal labs, controlled security simulations, CTF challenges, and independent learning.

Rather than simply documenting how an attack works, I aim to explore it from both offensive and defensive perspectives—understanding the underlying techniques, analyzing their behavior, investigating the evidence they leave behind, and discussing practical approaches for detection, response, and mitigation.

The content published here is intended to document my learning journey, share technical knowledge, and serve as a reference for others interested in cybersecurity.
